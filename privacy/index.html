Privacy policy

How Capital Bid Desk LLC handles information in its maintenance coordination service. Last updated November 17, 2026.

What we collect

Names, phone numbers and the contents of maintenance text messages and calls; repair details and photos you send; contractor prices and availability; owner approvals. Calls with contractors are made by an automated assistant that says so at the start of the call; calls may be transcribed, and the transcript is stored with the job.

How we use it

Only to coordinate maintenance for the property management company you rent from, work with, or own property through: collecting repair details, getting prices, scheduling, approvals and status updates. Automated suggestions, such as how urgent a request is, are stored with a short reason so staff can review them.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to complete a repair. For example, a booked contractor receives the address, access details and the tenant's phone number. We use service providers to send texts and calls and to process messages, under contract.

We do not sell personal information. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and security

Records are kept for as long as the property management company keeps the job history, then deleted. Access is limited to that company's staff and our operators. Message contents and phone numbers are stored in our database, not in system logs.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop texts. To see or delete your information, contact your property manager or noah@capitalbiddesk.com.
