What our bid checklist looks like

From a public Maryland Judiciary solicitation for accessibility consulting (K26-0050-25). Every package starts with a checklist like this, showing what is required, in what format, and who supplies it.

Required submittals for solicitation K26-0050-25
#SubmittalFormatWho handles it
1Transmittal letterAnyWe draft, you sign
2Executive summaryAnyYou supply substance, we assemble
3Attachment L, bidder profilePDF onlyWe complete
4Technical responseAnyYours
5Work planAnyYours
6Personnel experience and qualificationsAnyYou supply, we format
7Attachment M, bidder experiencePDF onlyYou supply projects, we write
8Attachment B, bid affidavitPDF onlyWe prepare, you sign
9IRS Form W-9AnyYou
10Attachment E, price form (Revision 1)Excel onlyWe set it up, you price it
11Intent to BidPortalWe track, you confirm

What the checklist flagged

  • The Intent to Bid was marked mandatory and due at the same minute as the bid itself.
  • Addendum 1 replaced the price form three weeks before close. Pricing on the original form meant a superseded submission.
  • Three attachments were PDF-only and the portal blocks the wrong file type.
  • All three addenda had to be acknowledged or the proposal could be rejected.

Frequently asked questions

Questions contractors ask us

How much does it cost?

A flat fee per bid, quoted up front based on the size of the contract, before any work starts. We never take a percentage of a contract you win.

Do you submit the bid for me?

No. You set the price, you sign and you submit. We prepare everything else and walk you through the submission so nothing gets rejected on format.

Which portals do you work with?

eMMA for Maryland state work, eVA for Virginia, OpenGov and Bonfire for many counties and agencies, plus the agency portals that run their own systems.

My company is not registered or not in good standing. Can you help?

Yes. We set up your vendor accounts, get your Maryland business standing in order, and help prepare certification applications such as Small Business Reserve and MBE. You sign, and state filing fees are paid directly to the state.

We are not based in Maryland, DC or Virginia. Can you still help?

Yes. We can prepare a bid package for a solicitation from any state. For firms bidding DMV work, we also handle registering your company in Maryland or Virginia, the vendor portals and local certification applications, and we watch DMV portals for you.

Can you guarantee I will win?

No one can. What we can do is make sure your bid is complete, on time and never thrown out for a missing form, and tell you honestly when a bid is not worth your week.

Looking at a bid right now?

Email the solicitation, or just your trade and the counties you work in. We reply within one business day with what it takes and a flat-fee quote.
