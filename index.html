What I do

01

Find the right opportunities

I watch eMMA, eVA, OpenGov, Bonfire and agency portals and send only what fits your trade, location and size.

02

Read the whole package

Every appendix and addendum. I flag disqualifiers early:

  • Mandatory pre-bid meetings
  • Years-in-business rules
  • License classes
  • Insurance limits
  • Set-aside conditions

03

Build the submittal

A checklist of every required document, the forms filled in, and the experience and certification sections written from your information. Nothing goes in without your approval.

04

Get you registered

eMMA, eVA, OpenGov, Bonfire, and your Maryland SDAT standing.

What stays yours

You set the price. You sign. You submit. I never take a percentage of a contract you win.

What one bid actually asked for

A recent Maryland state solicitation for a small specialist firm asked for 11 separate submittal items.

11 items. 9 required. 2 that actually needed the owner.

The other nine were forms, affidavits, certifications and formatting rules, three of them PDF-only with a portal that rejects the wrong file type. That is the part I take off your plate.
