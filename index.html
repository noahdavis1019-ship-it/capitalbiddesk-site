Government bids, prepared for you. You set the price and you sign.

Capital Bid Desk helps small firms win public work in Maryland, DC and Virginia. I find the solicitations that fit your business, read every page of them, and put the bid together, so you can stay on the job site instead of in the paperwork.

Send me a solicitation

Who this is for

Owner-run firms that can do the work but do not have a person whose job is bidding:

  • Trades and facilities contractors: HVAC, electrical, plumbing, painting, roofing, janitorial, grounds and snow
  • Specialist service firms, including digital accessibility, document remediation and Section 508 work
  • Certified small, minority and woman-owned businesses that want their certifications to count on a bid

What I do

Find the right opportunities

I watch the state, county and agency portals across Maryland, DC and Virginia, including eMMA, eVA, OpenGov and Bonfire, and send you only the solicitations that fit your trade, location and size.

Read the whole package

Every appendix and every addendum. I flag what disqualifies a bid before you spend time on it: mandatory pre-bid meetings, years-in-business rules, license classes, insurance limits, response-time requirements and set-aside conditions.

Build the submittal

A checklist of every required document, the forms filled in, and the experience and certification sections written up from your information. Nothing goes in that you have not approved.

Get you registered

If you are not set up to bid yet, I handle the registrations: eMMA, eVA, OpenGov, Bonfire, and keeping your Maryland SDAT standing current.

What stays yours

You set the price. You sign. You submit. I never take a percentage of a contract you win.

What that looks like on a real bid

A recent Maryland state solicitation for a small specialist firm asked for 11 separate submittal items.

11 items. 9 required. 2 that actually needed the owner.

The other nine were forms, affidavits, certifications and formatting rules, three of them PDF-only with a portal that rejects the wrong file type. That is the part I take off your plate.

How it works

  1. Send me the bid number or link. Or tell me your trade and counties and I will find them.
  2. I send back what it requires. The deadlines, the gates you must clear, and anything missing from your side.
  3. I assemble the package. You review it, set your price, sign and submit.

Finding opportunities covers Maryland, DC and Virginia. Reading a solicitation and building the response works for a bid from any state.
