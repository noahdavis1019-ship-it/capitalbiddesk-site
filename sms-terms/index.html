SMS terms

Maintenance text messages from Capital Bid Desk. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Program name: Capital Bid Desk Maintenance Texts.

Program description: transactional, conversational texts about residential repair requests, sent by Capital Bid Desk on behalf of property management companies. Tenants receive questions about a repair they reported and visit scheduling and status updates. Contractors receive job price requests and booking confirmations. Property owners receive repair price approval requests. We never send marketing or promotional messages.

Sent from: +1 (912) 228-3623.

Message frequency: varies with your repair requests or jobs, typically a few messages per request.

Cost: message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You will get one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe.

Help: reply HELP for help, or contact noah@capitalbiddesk.com or 310-912-4285.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How you opt in

  • Tenants opt in by texting the maintenance number to report a repair; the tenant always sends the first message. Our first reply confirms: "Capital Bid Desk: you'll get texts about your repair requests. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
  • Contractors opt in when they agree to receive job requests by text, including when our assistant asks on a phone call whether it may text them. The answer is recorded.
  • Property owners opt in through their management agreement with their property manager.

Consent and its date are stored with each contact. Anyone who replies STOP is not texted again unless they reply START.

Emergencies

Texting is not an emergency service. If anyone is in danger, call 911. Emergency repair reports are passed to a person at the property management company.

Privacy

See our privacy policy. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
