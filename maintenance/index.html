Maintenance coordination for rental properties

A service from Capital Bid Desk LLC. Tenants text one number to report a repair. Our assistant collects the details, gets prices from the property manager's own contractors, asks the owner to approve when needed, books the visit and keeps everyone updated. Staff approve by text, and emergencies always go to a person.

How it works

  • Tenants text the maintenance number to report a repair. We ask a few questions (what is wrong, where, whether it is still happening) and text them when a visit is booked.
  • Contractors get a text or a short phone call asking for a price and their first open date, then a booking confirmation with access details.
  • Property owners get a text asking them to approve a repair price when it is over the manager's approval limit.
  • Property managers see every job on a dashboard and get a text for anything that needs a person.
